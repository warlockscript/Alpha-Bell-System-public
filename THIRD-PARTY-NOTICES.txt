11. Text-to-speech voice models (Piper voices)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All voice models below were trained and published by the Piper voices project
(https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices). The licence stated for each is
the licence of its TRAINING DATASET, as recorded in that voice's MODEL_CARD.

Voice:   en_US-ljspeech-high (en_US-ljspeech-high.onnx + .onnx.json)
Dataset: LJ Speech Dataset — https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/
License: Public domain.
Note:    Trained from scratch (see "Derivation" below).

Voice:   en_US-libritts_r-medium (en_US-libritts_r-medium.onnx + .onnx.json)
Dataset: LibriTTS-R — http://www.openslr.org/141/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
         (licenses/CC-BY-4.0.txt). Attribution: LibriTTS-R corpus,
         Koizumi et al., derived from LibriTTS (Zen et al.) and
         LibriSpeech/LibriVox recordings.

Voice:   de_DE-thorsten-medium (de_DE-thorsten-medium.onnx + .onnx.json)
Dataset: Thorsten-Voice — https://github.com/thorstenMueller/Thorsten-Voice
License: CC0 1.0 Universal — public domain dedication
         (licenses/CC0-1.0.txt).

Voice:   es_ES-davefx-medium (es_ES-davefx-medium.onnx + .onnx.json)
Dataset: OHF-Voice voice-datasets —
         https://github.com/OHF-Voice/voice-datasets
License: CC0 1.0 Universal — public domain dedication
         (licenses/CC0-1.0.txt).

Voice:   fr_FR-siwis-medium (fr_FR-siwis-medium.onnx + .onnx.json)
Dataset: SIWIS French Speech Synthesis Database —
         https://datashare.is.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/2353
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
         (licenses/CC-BY-4.0.txt). Attribution: SIWIS French Speech
         Synthesis Database, University of Edinburgh / Idiap Research
         Institute.

Voice:   it_IT-paola-medium (it_IT-paola-medium.onnx + .onnx.json)
Dataset: Voice-Dataset-Italian —
         https://huggingface.co/datasets/paolapersico1/Voice-Dataset-Italian
License: CC0 1.0 Universal — public domain dedication
         (licenses/CC0-1.0.txt). The voice's MODEL_CARD defers to the
         dataset page, which declares "license: cc0-1.0".

Derivation
----------
Per their MODEL_CARDs, five of the six voices above were FINE-TUNED FROM the
Piper "en_US-lessac-medium" voice: libritts_r, thorsten, davefx, siwis and
paola. That base voice was trained on the Lessac corpus from the Blizzard
Challenge 2013, whose terms are published at
https://www.cstr.ed.ac.uk/projects/blizzard/2013/lessac_blizzard2013/license.html
and are NOT one of the standard open licences.

en_US-ljspeech-high is the only voice here trained from scratch, and is
therefore the only one carrying no dependency on that base model. It is the
voice used by default.
